# Product discovery

Do not write application code.

1. Read `.auto-company/product/product-brief.md`, the manifest, relevant existing product documents, analytics, user feedback, and current behavior.
2. For a new idea, delegate market and competitor research to `auto-company:product-researcher`. Require citations and include evidence against the idea.
3. Separate facts, user statements, assumptions, recommendations, and unknowns.
4. Identify the target customer, triggering situation, current workaround, job to be done, time-to-first-value, retention mechanism, buyer, pricing unit, and key cost drivers.
5. Produce at least two solution shapes and recommend the smallest wedge that can prove value.
6. Define measurable activation, core value, retention, revenue/efficiency, and kill/continue thresholds.
7. Update the product brief and feature contract with stable `BR`, `FR`, `NFR`, and `AC` identifiers.
8. Surface no more than five owner decisions. For each, provide a recommended default and consequence of choosing differently.

Reject vanity features that do not connect to an observable customer or business outcome.
